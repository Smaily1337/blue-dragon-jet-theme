What we offer

Comprehensive service support

Every BDJ machine is covered by full support — from warranty to spare parts, regular inspections and calibration.

  • In-warranty and post-warranty service

    We repair all BDJ models — free of charge under warranty; otherwise a transparent quote before we disassemble anything.

  • Remote diagnosis without shipping the machine

    Many faults are resolved by phone or video consultation — our technician guides you step by step, with no transport costs.

  • Periodic inspections and calibration

    Regular inspections and certified calibration ensure precision and reliability — your machine returns with a service report and a new warranty on repaired parts.
