Why Fibre Pusher – BDJ ROD PUSHER?

Fibre Pusher – BDJ ROD PUSHER

A hydraulic fibreglass pushing device equipped with speed and force adjustment as well as a metre counter, giving you full control over the fibre-pushing process.

Various pipe sizes can be accommodated thanks to interchangeable clamps. Mounted on a stable, corrosion-resistant frame, it can be tilted over a telecommunications manhole and secured to the manhole’s side walls. The hydraulic control system allows the fibre to be pushed in and withdrawn.

A hydraulic power unit is required to operate the machine.
